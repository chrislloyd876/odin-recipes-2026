Wheat Shake

Description

Made with sweetened condensed milk, whole milk, and puffed wheat cereal, this creamy, traditional treat is rich, comforting, and full of nostalgia.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup whole milk*
  • 3 tablespoons sweetened condensed milk
  • 3 tablespoons granulated sugar, or more to taste
  • 1 cup puffed wheat cereal
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1/2 cup ice

Steps

  1. Add the milk, sweetened condensed milk, sugar, puffed wheat cereal, salt, and ice to a high-speed blender. Blend on high until smooth and creamy, about 1-2 minute.
  2. If you prefer a thinner consistency, you can add a splash more milk. The puffed wheat cereal will mostly dissolve, giving the milkshake its signature texture.
  3. Pour into glasses and serve immediately. Enjoy on its own or with your favorite Cuban sandwich.
